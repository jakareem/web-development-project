About this site

I started Futures as a personal project to teach myself the mechanics of futures trading before I risk any real money. Rather than read a textbook cover to cover, I wanted a small, working site where the ideas I read about (ticks, margin, leverage) turn into something I can calculate myself.

This is not a brokerage, a signal service, or investment advice. It's a study aid I'm building and improving as I learn more.

What's here

Calculator

A profit and loss calculator for a handful of common futures contracts, so I can check my intuition against the real math.

Learning path

The order I'm working through as a beginner, from vocabulary to paper trading to a written trading plan.

Glossary

Short, plain-language definitions of the terms I kept having to look up.

Who I am

I'm the sole author of this site, learning futures trading as a hobby and building this project alongside that. If you have a correction or a suggestion, use the contact page.

Futures is my educational project. Contract specifications can change, so I recommend verifying them with the exchange.
