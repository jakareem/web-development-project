What Are Futures?

Futures are contracts that lock in a price today for a transaction that will happen later. They are used for commodities, indexes, and currencies.

Key Concepts

  • Contract: A standardized agreement traded on an exchange.
  • Margin: A small deposit used to open a futures position.
  • Leverage: Allows traders to control more value with less money.
  • Expiration: The date the contract ends.
